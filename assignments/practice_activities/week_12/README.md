## What the acronyms mean in the notebook

- **ETV** — Extract (read a source, cache it with its access date), Transform (clean, join, filter, compute; named steps with checks), Visualize (one figure per question).
- **SSVA** — Summary Statistic → Visualization → Analysis: name the number before choosing the chart, then write the sentence the chart has to support.

## Headline numbers (cached data, accessed 2026-11-10)

53 Hall of Famers are listed at running back, halfback or fullback (17 pre-modern two-way players); a careless `str.contains("RB", case=False)` matches 127 rows, mostly cornerbacks and quarterbacks. 8 Hall of Famers played in 1999 or later (Thomas, Smith, Bettis, Faulk, Martin, Davis, James, Tomlinson); only James and Tomlinson have complete careers in the data; Thurman Thomas never reaches 50 carries in an observed season. 4,619 RB / FB player-seasons over 27 seasons; 1,841 qualified at ≥ 50 carries, 56–77 peers per season. 2006: 59 qualified backs, median 745 rushing yards; Tomlinson 1,815 = 1,070 above the median, 2.4× the median, 100th percentile. Among backs with ≥ 5 qualified seasons (153): the 6 Hall of Famers have a median season at the 79th percentile and a median 36 % of seasons at or above the 90th, against the 61st percentile and 0 % for the others. Henry: 10 qualified seasons, median 98th, 70 % at or above the 90th; McCaffrey 9 / 89th / 44 %; Gore 16 / 84th; Peterson (the Vikings one; the name has two ids) 12 / 93rd / 70 %. Sensitivity at 25 / 50 / 100 carries: 89 / 70 / 46 peers per season; Hall of Fame median 79th / 75th / 67th; others 39th / 40th / 40th.
